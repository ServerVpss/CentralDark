Pivete, pra puxar a lista de modelos online no gateway, usa o endpoint /v1/models:



curl -s https://api.centralldark.com/v1/models \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $CENTRALLDARK_API_KEY" \
  -H "Accept: application/json" | jq '.data[].id'




Se a tua chave for do tipo x-api-key:



curl -s https://api.centralldark.com/v1/models \
  -H "x-api-key: $CENTRALLDARK_API_KEY" \
  -H "Accept: application/json" | jq '.data[].id'




Se tiver o darkai instalado, é mais fácil ainda:



darkai --list-models
